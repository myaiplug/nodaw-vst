In this demo vs the plugin

What’s real here.

  • PortedThe engine: Amp, the 7-band EQ with per-band Solo and Mute, the sub shelf, HPF/LPF, the ping-pong delay, FDN reverb and stereo flanger returns, and width.
  • ParamsAll 57 parameters plus bypass, with the plugin’s own ranges, units and curves (58 host parameters in the plugin).
  • PresetsAll 21 factory presets, by name and value, read from the plugin’s preset files.
  • A/BBypass uses the engine’s own crossfade to the latency-delayed dry signal, so the switch never clicks.
  • Plugin onlyThe plugin’s analyser with draggable band nodes, effect LEDs and the two faceplates
  • Plugin onlyDAW automation, MIDI learn, undo/redo, A/B slots and user presets
  • StatusThe Harmonic Space plugin (VST3 + CLAP, Windows x64) is in development: unreleased, not for sale, no price yet.
Signal flow

Harmonic Space, stage by stage.

Amp→7-band EQ→Sub shelf→HPF / LPF→Delay · Reverb · Flanger→Width→Output→Mix

Band Solo and Mute

Solo auditions a band’s region with a crossfaded band-pass (or low/high-pass on the shelves). Mute takes the band to −36 dB.

Recipes by family

Presets are grouped as Vocals, Low end, Space, Character and Fix-it, converted with the prototype’s own knob mappings.

Dark or brushed aluminium

The analyser shows the whole shape with a node per band plus the filter nodes, effect activity LEDs and a correlation meter.

EQ

60 Hz low shelf, five bells and a 16 kHz high shelf, then 12 dB/oct high- and low-pass.

Reverb

An FDN reverb with decay, size, pre-delay and damping.

Delay

A feedback delay with tone and ping-pong, alongside a stereo flanger.
