Install

  1. Run NoDAW-Plugins-win64-trial-setup.exe and pick the plugins you want. It puts the VST3s in C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3 and the CLAPs in C:\Program Files\Common Files\CLAP.
  2. Or unzip and copy the .vst3 folders and .clap files to those folders yourself.
  3. Rescan plugins in your DAW. The 14-day trial starts the first time each plugin opens.
  4. After buying, open the plugin, click Activate and paste the license key from your receipt.
